Process

From first call to first cover.

Every engagement runs the same sequence. The calendar is built around your installs, shoots and launches, not the other way around.

  1. Week 1

    Audit call

    Sixty minutes on your portfolio, your numbers and the inquiries you actually get.

  2. Week 2

    Diagnosis

    A written read of what is true about your brand right now, and what is holding it back.

  3. Weeks 3–4

    The plan

    A twelve-month calendar tied to installs, photography and product drops.

  4. Month 2 on

    Production

    Shoots on install day, writing, editing and pitching, approved by you and run by us.

  5. Quarterly

    Review

    We report against inquiries, placements and sell-through, then adjust the plan.
