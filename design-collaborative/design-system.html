A quiet architecture
of type, light & line

The site behaves like a monograph, not a brochure. Photography carries the emotion; type is large, light and unhurried; structure comes from hairlines and generous margins. A drawn line, borrowed from the firm's construction sets, appears wherever we talk about how the work gets built.

Palette

Warm, mineral, and low-chroma, so photography always leads. Ivory is the page, Paper the alternate surface, and Espresso the dark chapter. Clay is the single accent: it is reserved for numerals, hover states, and ornament. Use Clay Deep when the accent sits on small text.

Grid & rhythm

A 12-column grid with 4.2vw outer margins, collapsing to a single column under 960px. Sections breathe at 96–200px vertically. Compositions are deliberately off-centre: text holds 5–6 columns while images take 6–7 and bleed to the edge.

Principles

The rules that keep every page in the same world, whoever builds it next.

  • Photography leads. Shoot in natural light. Alternate wide rooms with close details (joinery, stone, tile, hardware). Never use stock imagery.
  • Copy is never trimmed for looks. Layout makes long text feel light, through ledes, measure, and pacing, rather than hiding it.
  • One H1, logical H2/H3. Decorative numerals and captions are hidden from assistive tech, and every heading is real text.
  • Motion is slow and single. Reveal once, ease out over 1.2–1.5s, never loop. Everything is visible without JavaScript and with reduced motion.
  • Images are sized and described. Use descriptive file names and alt text, explicit dimensions, and lazy-load anything below the fold.
  • No cards, no icons, no carousels. Use lists, hairlines, and composition instead.
