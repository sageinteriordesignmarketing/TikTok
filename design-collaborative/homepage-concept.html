From the First Drawings to the Final Detail

Design Collaborative is a boutique, full service interior design firm based in San Luis Obispo, with a second office in the Boise area. For 35 years, we've designed homes, hotels, wineries, and workplaces, many of them for clients we've worked with before.

We work alongside your architect and contractor from the early stages, then draw out every decision in a complete set of construction plans. Your builder knows exactly what to build and what it will cost, so nothing gets guessed or redone on site.

Beautiful design still needs a path to construction

Your builder builds what's on the drawings. Your architect's plans cover the walls and wiring, but not the inside. Without interior plans, the crew fills in the blanks with standard options or their best interpretation of what you wanted.

Our NCIDQ-certified team draws every detail, down to the cabinets and lighting, so nothing gets left to guesswork. Builders tell us our plans are the most complete they receive.

For people building the space they've been planning for years

Our residential clients are often building a place to retire or the house their grown kids and grandkids will come back to. Many own more than one property, so quality and low maintenance matter as much as looks. Our commercial and hospitality clients want the same for their businesses: every square foot utilized, and a team that does its job well.

Our best projects start with your architect and contractor at the table with us from day one. If your plans are underway, reach out before they're final.
