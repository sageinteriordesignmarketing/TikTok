# Weekly Team Update (Slack #sage-team)

How to draft Kyra's weekly update to the team. Saved from Kyra's edits on Nov 23, 2026. Follow this every time.

## When and where
- Channel: **#sage-team** (Slack channel ID `C08LY90B7S4`).
- Post before the Monday team sync. Team sync is **Monday 2 pm ET**.
- Always save it as a **Slack draft** in Kyra's account. Never send it. Kyra edits and sends.
- After the update, the team reviews what it says, changes anything that's wrong, then updates the RYG Doc.

## What to read first (last 7 days)
1. **#sage-team:** RYG posts from Campbell (social clients), Bella (Think Chic, LMI, BCC) and Hailey (DC), plus Kyra's asks and anyone's questions.
2. **#eod-reports:** wins, content counts and anything under "Do you need help?"
3. If a client has no fresh RYG, use their last rating and ask the owner for an update in the message.

## Format
Kyra's style: short, warm, plain. Italic section headers with no emoji. No extra sign-offs.

```
Here's this week's update since we're [skipping sync today / meeting at 2 pm ET] :slightly_smiling_face:

_Shoutouts_
• @person for [one specific thing]. [Optional: one short line on why it mattered.]
...
_RYG review_
• _Client (Color):_ One or two sentences: what's happening and the next step or owner.
...
_Updates_
• _Topic:_ one or two sentences.
• _Next team sync:_ Monday 2 pm ET
Thank you all!!
```

## Kyra's edits (what to do differently)
- **Open simply.** No holiday greeting line. Start with "Here's this week's update..." and the 🙂 emoji.
- **Italic headers, no emojis on them** (`_Shoutouts_`, `_RYG review_`, `_Updates_`).
- **Keep shoutouts short.** One specific thing per person. Cut the extra explanation after it (she removed "more of her personal project stories, fewer generic tips").
- **Credit the people, not just the client win.** A client milestone (e.g. TKS hitting 10K followers) is a shoutout *to the team members who ran that account* (Bella and Campbell). Group shared wins into one bullet for both people.
- **Close with "Thank you all!!"** and nothing else (no "so grateful for this team" or heart).
- **Leave decisions to Kyra** with a `[KYRA: ...]` placeholder. Don't guess her answer.
- RYG and Updates sections were kept as written: one line per client with a clear next step and an @mention for the owner.

## Standing rules and answers
- **Content deadline:** the team keeps creating content until it's sent to clients on the **15th** of the month. If someone asks for deadline flexibility, remind them of this unless Kyra says otherwise.
- **Estimates aren't results.** A pending or estimated project (e.g. "possible $300K," "estimated $250K to $260K") stays "possible" until the contract signs. Say so in the RYG line.
- Never name the designer's clients (homeowners) in a way that leaves the team channel, and never put fees or margins in the update.
- Every client in the RYG sheet gets a line. If one is missing a rating, @ the owner to add it.
