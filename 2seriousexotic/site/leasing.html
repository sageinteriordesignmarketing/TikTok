Leasing,
engineered

A lease built for exotics looks different from one built for a sedan. Here's how we structure them, and why the numbers come out better.

Two structures

Open-end
or closed-end

Both come with early exit and trade-out options. The difference is who carries the residual at the end, and how much flexibility you want along the way.

Best for business owners & operators

Open-end

Lower payments in exchange for taking on the end-of-term value. Often paired with a balloon, so more of your capital stays in the business.

  • Lower monthly payment
  • Optional balloon to reduce payments further
  • Pay off at any time, including the balloon
  • Flexible mileage
Best for drivers who want certainty

Closed-end

A fixed payment and a known exit. Hand the car back at the end of the term, or trade into the next one.

  • Predictable monthly cost
  • Walk away or trade up at term end
  • Early exit and trade-out options
  • Mileage from 2,000 a year
The residual

Where the
savings live

Your payment mostly covers the car's drop in value over the term. Banks use generic residual tables, which assume a Lamborghini loses value like a family car. We set residuals on how exotics actually depreciate, so you pay for less of the car.

  • Terms24–60 months
  • MileageFrom 2,000 / yr
  • Typical down15–20%

FAQ

Straight
answers

Something not covered? Call 954.594.3642 and you'll speak to us directly.

What are standard lease terms?

Our leases typically run 24 to 60 months, with flexible mileage options starting as low as 2,000 miles per year. Terms can be tailored for collectors, rental operators or business owners.

What's the minimum down payment?

Most structures require 15–20% down, depending on the vehicle, your profile and the deal structure. For qualified clients, we can design creative alternatives to a standard down payment.

How are residual values set?

Residuals are tailored to the real-world depreciation curves of exotic vehicles, not cookie-cutter numbers from traditional banks. That keeps things fair and flexible when it's time to upgrade or exit.

Can I exit my lease early?

Yes. Whether you choose an open-end or closed-end lease, we offer early exit and trade-out options for clients who want to upgrade or change vehicles before the end of the term. Each structure has different benefits, and we'll match the flexibility to your goals and your car.

Can I lease an older or pre-owned exotic?

Yes. Recent deals include a 2007 Lamborghini Gallardo Spyder and a 2016 Ferrari 488. Tell us the car and we'll tell you how it can be structured.

What is a balloon payment?

A balloon is a set amount due at the end of the term, which lowers your monthly payment. You can pay off the lease, including the balloon, at any time. On our McLaren Artura deal, a $63,700 balloon kept the payment at $3,894 a month over 60 months.
