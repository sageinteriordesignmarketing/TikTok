The pain point

Beautiful work. Invisible to the people searching for it.

Heather Peterson Design creates eclectic, colourful interiors built around antique heritage pieces. The work was there. Google just wasn’t sending the right people to it.

This is the situation we see most often with interior designers. The portfolio is strong, but the website was never set up to be found by someone who doesn’t already know your name. Here’s where things stood in March 2024:

  • #79Google position for “interior design Minneapolis.” That’s page eight, where almost no one looks.
  • 94Estimated organic visits a month, down 10% at the time (Semrush).
  • 964Keywords the site ranked for, many from older blog posts that brought in readers who were never going to hire a designer.
  • 12Keywords related to “interior design” — the searches that actually lead to clients.

The desire

Be the designer Minneapolis finds first.

Searches for her name were already working: “heather peterson design” was the site’s top query. The next step was to show up when a homeowner types “interior designer Minneapolis” without knowing her name, and to stop ranking for searches that don’t lead to projects.

The work

Fewer, better signals.

  1. I

    Optimize the site

    Website optimizations, finished at the end of May 2024, aimed at local, high-intent searches.

  2. II

    Clean up old content

    Older blog content that ranked for the wrong things was cleaned up, so the site stopped attracting visitors who would never become clients.

  3. III

    Keep what works

    Evergreen posts that were already earning steady traffic were kept and left to keep performing.

“Keywords went down, and that was the point.”

Fewer keywords, better ones.

Total keywords fell from 964 to 778 between March and December. That sounds like a loss. It wasn’t: the ones that dropped came from old blog content ranking for searches that never turned into projects. Meanwhile, keywords tied to “interior design” more than doubled, and the site’s Semrush authority score held steady at 19 while rankings rose.

The blogs that kept earning.

The cleanup kept the posts that were already working. These were still bringing in steady search traffic in December:

  • How to hang and stack art on a wall
  • How to mix metals
  • Tricks of the trade for pairs of twin beds
  • Ghost chair cushions
